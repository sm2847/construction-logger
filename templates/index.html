Welcome to the live monitoring platform for the GD1 Offsite Sustainable Construction Project 2026. We have been tasked with the construction of a 13m footbridge across the West Cambridge creek working with Laing O’Rourke as the contractor and Ramboll as the consultant. The main construction phase will run from Wednesday 20th May to Friday 22nd May 2026.

This platform has been set up to help monitor site activity, track progress in real time, and compare planned works against what is actually happening on site throughout the project.

📊 Live Progress Tracking
The progress display on the left shows live updates based on real-time site activity and completed construction tasks as work progresses.

📅 Planned Programme
The baseline schedule outlines the planned sequence of activities, expected durations, and allocated resources prepared by the planning and scheduling team before construction began.

📝 Activity Logger
The activity logger should be used by Team Leads and the Construction Manager to record completed works, ongoing activities, and any delays or changes to the planned schedule. This allows the Digital Engineering Team to monitor productivity, identify trends, and compare planned progress with actual site performance.

🔎 Digital Site Monitoring
By combining live data, site monitoring tools, and digital reporting systems, the platform provides a central view of project performance and supports day-to-day decision making during construction.

Fibre Optics Structural Health Monitoring

🔍 Steel Beam Strain Analysis

To evaluate structural performance under load variations, advanced fibre optic strain gauges were deployed along one of the two main steel beams of the bridge.

💡 Sensor Distribution Matrix:
• Top Flange Array: Real-time compression tracking.
• Bottom Flange Array: Tension load mapping during lifting operations.

This instrumentation establishes live health indexing safety margins. Plotted and optimized by Darong.
